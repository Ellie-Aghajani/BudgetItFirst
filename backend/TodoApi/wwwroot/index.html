Budget It First

This is the server behind the Budget It First app. The app itself lives at budgetitfirst.com.

Open the app

How to use the app

  1. JoinCreate a free account with your email or Google.
  2. Set your budgetEnter your monthly income and plan your categories.
  3. Add shopping itemsList what you need to buy, with a category and estimated cost.
  4. Check off and logTick items as you buy them and log what you actually paid.
  5. Track and adjustWatch what's left on the dashboard and calendar, get alerts, and adjust your plan anytime.

For developers: endpoints are under /api and require a signed-in Firebase token. /api/health shows whether the server and database are up.
